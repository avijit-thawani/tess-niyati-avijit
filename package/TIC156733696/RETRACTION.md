# Retraction (version 2, 2026-10-13): "Avijit" on TYC 717-2552-1 (TIC 156733696)

**The signal is retracted as a planet candidate on TYC 717-2552-1.**

Vetting completed after version 1 (2026-10-09) shows that the dimming is not on the target star, and that the signal fails several independent checks:

- **Pixel-level difference imaging** (3 sectors, 32 transits) places the missing light **47.1″ south-east of the target (21.6σ)**. That is on or near Gaia DR3 3323296950337617408 (G = 15.7, 43.8″), which fits better than the target by Δχ² = 863. Making the observed dip would need a ~24% eclipse on that star. The measured depth also rises steeply with aperture size.
- **Transit fit:** the dip is far too long for this star, with free-fit stellar density 0.13 vs 1.71 g/cm³ catalogued.
- **Hold-out seasons:** in the 2021 season the signal is absent at the predicted times (11 ± 56 ppm).
- **Second reduction:** NASA TESS-SPOC (550 ppm) and MIT QLP (372 ppm) disagree, and sectors disagree within each pipeline.
- **Unaffected:** the host star itself looks clean, with Gaia RUWE 0.97 and no non-single-star flag.

**Most likely explanation:** a blended eclipsing binary on a neighbouring star.

Full vetting outputs and the checklist used are at https://github.com/avijit-thawani/tess-niyati-avijit
